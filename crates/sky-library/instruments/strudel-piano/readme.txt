Strudel's piano pack

The recordings are the Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, a
Yamaha C5 grand: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. Credit it as
"Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0)".

Strudel plays its eighth velocity layer, encoded as MP3, from the piano
pack in felixroos/dough-samples (piano.json). Skyware Sound downloads
those 29 files, each checked against the SHA-256 it lists, and plays them
as one velocity layer with piano.sfz here, written for Skyware Sound: each
key plays the recording nearest its note, as in Strudel.
